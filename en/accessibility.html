Accessibility Statement

Ohr Foundation is committed to making its website accessible to all users, with dignity, equality, and usability in mind.

Our Accessibility Commitment

We place great importance on making the website accessible for people with disabilities and continuously improving the browsing experience.

Accessibility Adjustments on the Website

  • Clear and structured navigation
  • Proper heading hierarchy and semantic content structure
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Accessibility options through the site accessibility button
  • Responsive support for mobile and tablet devices

Accessibility Contact

If you encounter an accessibility issue on the website, please contact us at ohr.foundation.aca@gmail.com or call 04-9562576.
